## How to use

1. **Switch the fan off** — Make sure the fan has fully stopped.
2. **Shape the head** — Bend the duster head into a curve that fits over the blade.
3. **Slide along each blade** — Hook it over the blade and pull it gently towards you.
4. **Shake off the dust** — Shake the head outdoors or over a bin.

## Care

Shake out after each use. If the head is removable, wash it in mild soapy water and let it air dry before the next use.
