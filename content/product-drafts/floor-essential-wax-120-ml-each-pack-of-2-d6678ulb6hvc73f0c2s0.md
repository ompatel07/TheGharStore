## How to use

1. **Clean and dry** — Dust the surface and let it dry completely.
2. **Test first** — Try a small hidden spot to check the finish.
3. **Apply thinly** — Put a little polish on a soft, lint-free cloth and wipe along the grain of the wood.
4. **Wait, then buff** — Leave it for a few minutes, then buff with a clean dry cloth until no residue remains.

## Care

Close the cap tightly. Store away from heat and sunlight, out of reach of children.

## Safety

Buff floors well — leftover wax can make floors slippery. Don’t use on tiles, marble or stone.

## FAQs

**Can I use it on tiles or marble?**
No. It’s made for wood. On tiles or marble it can leave a slippery film.

**How often should I polish?**
Every few weeks for furniture you use daily, or whenever the wood looks dull.

**Will it fix scratches?**
It can make light surface scratches less visible. Deep scratches need a wood filler or touch-up.
