## How to use

1. **Clean the surface** — Wipe the spot clean and let it dry completely. Adhesive won’t hold on dust, oil or damp walls.
2. **Stick and press** — Peel the backing, place the holder and press firmly for about 30 seconds.
3. **Wait before loading** — Give the adhesive time to set (ideally overnight) before hanging anything.
4. **Fit the rod** — Clip the rod into the holder. Keep loads light.

## Care

Don’t hang heavy items. Avoid rough, textured or freshly painted walls. To remove, peel slowly from one corner; a hair-dryer on low heat can soften the adhesive.

## FAQs

**Will it hold a heavy curtain?**
These are for light rods and light curtains. For heavy curtains, use screwed brackets.

**Does it work in the bathroom?**
Yes, on smooth tiles. Stick it when the wall is completely dry and wait before using the shower.

**Will it damage my wall?**
On tiles and glass it usually peels off cleanly. On painted walls, removal can lift paint.
