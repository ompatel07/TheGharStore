## How to use

1. **Mount it** — Stick it on a clean, dry, smooth wall or tile. Wait before loading knives (ideally overnight).
2. **Sharpen** — Hold the handle and draw the blade through the sharpener from heel to tip, 5–6 times, with light pressure.
3. **Store** — Wipe the blade and slide the knife into the holder.
