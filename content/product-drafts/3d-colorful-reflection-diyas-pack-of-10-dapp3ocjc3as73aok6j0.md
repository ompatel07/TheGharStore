## How to use

1. **Fit the wick** — Push a cotton wick through the metal holder.
2. **Centre the holder** — Place the holder in the centre of the diya and check the metal tube stands straight.
3. **Add water, then oil** — Pour about one spoon of water first, then oil on top. The water layer keeps the base cooler.
4. **Light it** — Light the wick and place the diya on a stable, heat-safe surface.

## Care

Let diyas cool completely before touching or moving them. Wipe off oil with a soft cloth and warm soapy water. Store in the box so they don’t scratch.

## Safety

This is an open flame. Never leave a lit diya unattended. Keep it away from curtains, paper and rangoli powder, and out of reach of children and pets. Always add water before oil.

## FAQs

**Are these LED?**
No — they use oil and a wick, like a traditional diya. If you want flame-free diyas, see our water-sensor LED diyas.

**Why add water?**
A spoon of water under the oil keeps the base cooler and helps the diya last longer.

**Can I reuse them?**
Yes. Clean them after they cool and replace the wick next time.
