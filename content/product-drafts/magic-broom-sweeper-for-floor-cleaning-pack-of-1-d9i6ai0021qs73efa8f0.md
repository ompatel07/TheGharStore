## How to use

1. **Set it up** — Fit the pole into the head (if packed separately) and set the height that’s comfortable for you.
2. **Angle the head** — Turn the head to the angle you need — flat for open floor, angled for corners and under furniture.
3. **Push in one direction** — Push water or dust in long strokes towards the drain, door or a dustpan. Lift the blade between strokes for a cleaner finish.
4. **Rinse and hang** — Rinse the blade, shake off the water and hang the wiper by its loop to dry.

## Care

Rinse the rubber blade after use and let it dry. Hang it up rather than leaving it standing on the blade, so the edge stays straight. Keep it away from sharp objects that could nick the rubber.

## FAQs

**Does it work on dry floors?**
Yes, it can push loose hair, crumbs and dust into a pile. For fine dust on large areas a broom or vacuum may still be quicker.

**Is it safe for marble and wooden floors?**
The blade is soft rubber, which is gentle on smooth hard floors like marble and tiles. On wooden floors, avoid leaving standing water.

**Can I use it on glass?**
You can use the blade on large glass doors or windows. Use light pressure and a clean blade.

**Is it a broom?**
No — it’s a rubber-blade wiper (squeegee). It works best for pushing water and loose debris, not for sweeping rough surfaces.
