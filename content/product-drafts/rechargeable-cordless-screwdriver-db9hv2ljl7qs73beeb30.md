## How to use

1. **Charge it fully** — Charge the screwdriver with the cable before first use.
2. **Fit a bit** — Push the right bit firmly into the chuck.
3. **Choose the direction** — Use the switch to set forward (tighten) or reverse (loosen).
4. **Drive the screw** — Hold the screwdriver straight in line with the screw and press the trigger. Finish the last turn gently so you don’t strip the screw head.
