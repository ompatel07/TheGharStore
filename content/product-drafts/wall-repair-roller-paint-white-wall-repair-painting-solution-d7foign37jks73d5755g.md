## Description

Small marks on a white wall don’t need a full repaint. This touch-up roller keeps the paint and the roller together: squeeze the bottle, and paint flows to a small foam roller you can run straight over the mark.

It’s made for quick fixes — crayon lines, scuff marks near switches, small patches after removing a nail or hook — on walls that are already white. Because there’s no tray or open tin, it’s easy to do a two-minute fix and put it away.

The roller head comes off for rinsing, so you can use the same bottle again for the next touch-up.

## How to use

1. **Clean the spot** — Wipe the area with a dry cloth so it’s free of dust and grease. Let it dry fully.
2. **Prime the roller** — Hold the bottle upright and squeeze gently until the roller is evenly wet. Roll it on a piece of paper first to spread the paint.
3. **Roll over the mark** — Roll back and forth over the mark with light pressure. Thin coats look better than one thick coat.
4. **Let it dry, then repeat** — Let the first coat dry completely (follow the drying time on the bottle) and add a second coat if the mark still shows.
5. **Rinse and close** — Pull off the roller, rinse it under running water, let it dry and close the cap tightly.

## Care

Close the cap tightly after every use so the paint doesn’t dry in the nozzle. Rinse the roller with water while the paint is still wet. Store upright, away from direct sun and out of reach of children.

## FAQs

**Will it match my wall colour?**
Only if your wall is white. The paint is white, so on coloured or off-white walls the patch may show. Try it on a small hidden spot first.

**Can it fill deep cracks or holes?**
It’s for surface marks and small patches. Deep cracks or holes need wall putty or filler first; you can use this roller to paint over the dried filler.

**How many coats will I need?**
Light marks usually need one or two thin coats. Dark marks such as pen or crayon may need more. Let each coat dry before the next.

**Can I use it again later?**
Yes. Rinse the roller after use and close the cap tightly. Paint that’s left open will dry out.

**Does it smell strongly?**
Paints of this kind usually have a mild smell. Keep the room airy while it dries.
