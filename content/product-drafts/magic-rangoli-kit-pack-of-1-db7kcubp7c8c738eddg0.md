## How to use

1. **Place the stencil** — Put the stencil on a clean, dry, flat floor.
2. **Add colour** — Sprinkle rangoli colour evenly over the open parts of the stencil.
3. **Lift straight up** — Lift the stencil straight up so the edges stay sharp.
4. **Finish your way** — Add a border, diyas or flowers around the design.
