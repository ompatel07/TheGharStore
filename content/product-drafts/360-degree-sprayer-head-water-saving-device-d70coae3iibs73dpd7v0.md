## Description

Set it on the lawn, connect your hose and turn on the tap. The three arms spin to spread water in a full circle, so plants get an even soak while you do something else.

Turn the nozzles to change the spray: a lower angle spins faster and sprays wider; an upright angle spins slower and waters a smaller area.

## How to use

1. **Connect the hose** — Attach your garden hose to the connector and check it’s tight.
2. **Set the angle** — Turn the nozzles: lower for wider spray, upright for a smaller area.
3. **Place and turn on** — Set the sprinkler on level ground and open the tap slowly.
4. **Move as needed** — Move it around the garden to cover all beds.
