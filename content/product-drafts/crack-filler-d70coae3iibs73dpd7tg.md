## How to use

1. **Clean and dry** — Remove old grout, dirt and soap scum from the gap. Let it dry fully.
2. **Cut the nozzle** — Cut the tip at an angle — a smaller cut gives a thinner line.
3. **Fill the gap** — Squeeze gently and move steadily along the gap.
4. **Smooth and wipe** — Smooth the line with a damp finger or spatula and wipe off extra right away.
5. **Let it set** — Keep the area dry until fully set (check the time on the tube; usually about a day).
