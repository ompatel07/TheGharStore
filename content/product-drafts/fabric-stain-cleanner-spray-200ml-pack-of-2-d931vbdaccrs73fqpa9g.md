## How to use

1. **Test first** — Spray a small hidden area and blot with a white cloth to check the colour doesn’t run.
2. **Spray the mark** — Spray lightly from about 15–20 cm.
3. **Work it in** — Gently rub with a soft brush or cloth from the outside of the mark inwards.
4. **Blot dry** — Blot with a clean, dry cloth. Let the fabric air-dry completely.

## Care

Store upright, tightly closed, away from heat and direct sun. Keep out of reach of children. Don’t use on leather, silk or dry-clean-only fabrics.

## FAQs

**Will it remove old stains?**
Fresh marks come off most easily. Old or set-in stains may lighten but not disappear fully.

**Can I use it on leather?**
No. Use it on fabric only.
