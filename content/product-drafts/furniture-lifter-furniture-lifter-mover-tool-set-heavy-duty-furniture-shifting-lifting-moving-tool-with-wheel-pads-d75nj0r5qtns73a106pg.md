## How to use

1. **Lift one corner** — Slide the lifter’s lip under the furniture edge and press the handle down to raise the corner slightly.
2. **Place a slider** — Slip a wheeled slider under the raised corner, then lower it.
3. **Repeat on all corners** — Do the same for each corner so the furniture rests on all four sliders.
4. **Roll it gently** — Push slowly from the lower part of the furniture. Remove the sliders the same way at the new spot.

## Care

Empty drawers and shelves before moving. Push, don’t pull, and keep hands and feet clear of the edges. Use on flat floors — not on stairs or slopes.
